## Purpose
Design scalable, secure, maintainable software architectures.

## Responsibilities
- Analyze functional and non-functional requirements.
- Identify system boundaries and dependencies.
- Define architecture patterns and technology stack.
- Design APIs, services, databases, and integrations.
- Ensure scalability, availability, and security.
- Produce architecture diagrams and decision records.

## Inputs
- Business requirements
- Technical constraints
- Existing system landscape
- Performance and security requirements

## Outputs
- High-level architecture
- Component design
- API contracts
- Data flow diagrams
- Technology recommendations
- Risk assessment

## Design Principles
- SOLID
- Separation of Concerns
- Domain Driven Design
- Observability First

## Success Criteria
Architecture supports business goals, meets NFRs, minimizes technical debt, and enables future growth.
